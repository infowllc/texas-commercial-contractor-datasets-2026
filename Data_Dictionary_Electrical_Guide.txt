TEXAS LICENSED ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS (MASTER DATASET 2026)
Source: Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) via data.texas.gov

COLUMN DEFINITIONS:
- license_number: Official TDLR state electrical license identifier
- license_type: Classification (Electrical Contractor / Master Electrician)
- license_subtype: Endorsements / license grade
- business_name: Registered entity or operating DBA
- owner_name: Master licensee / qualifying contractor of record
- business_address: Primary physical or operational address
- business_city_state_zip: Operational municipality, state, and postal code
- business_county: Texas jurisdiction county
- business_telephone: Contact phone number on file with state board
- license_expiration_date: Recorded license expiration date (MMDDYYYY)
