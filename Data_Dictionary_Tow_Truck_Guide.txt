TEXAS LICENSED TOW TRUCK & RECOVERY OPERATORS (MASTER DATASET 2026)
Source: Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) via data.texas.gov

COLUMN DEFINITIONS:
- license_number: Official state TDLR operating license identifier
- license_type: Classification (Incident Management / Consent Tow / Private Property)
- license_subtype: Endorsements / operating credentials
- business_name: Registered operating entity or DBA
- operator_name: Licensed operator / qualifying individual of record
- business_address: Primary physical or dispatch address
- business_city_state_zip: Operational municipality, state, and postal code
- business_county: Texas jurisdiction county
- business_telephone: Contact phone number on file with state board
- license_expiration_date: Official license expiration date (MMDDYYYY)
